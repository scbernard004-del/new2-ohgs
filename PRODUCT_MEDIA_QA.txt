OHGS v42 — PRODUCT SPOTLIGHT AND ARUSHA SHOWROOM
14 November 2026 | Release: v42-product-spotlight-showroom

What customers see
- Four OHGS cards immediately after the homepage hero, with clean, cropped photos, model details, and a product-specific WhatsApp enquiry in English or Kiswahili.
- The same products are searchable in Products. Makita, EDON and INGCO also appear in Power Tools; WALKLANDER appears in Construction Materials.
- Four product/range pages have their own specifications, translated titles, support links and sitemap entries.
- The 40.7-second uploaded tour is the main Home hero video and is also on Products, Gallery and Outdoor Power. It shows a mower near the shop on Uhuru Road, the entrance, drills, hand tools and workshop equipment. The older showroom tour is still in Gallery.
- Playback starts muted when the video comes into view, where the browser permits. Use Sound on for audio. A poster and Play control remain available.

Product information and photography
- Makita 4350CT: Makita confirms 720 W, 26 mm stroke, 800–2,800 strokes/min, 135 mm wood and 10 mm steel capacity, bevel up to 45° left/right, and 2.7 kg net weight. Reference: https://www.makita.co.th/V2Products/Details/2407
- EDON AD-18: the supplied flyer lists 18 V, 10 mm chuck, 30 N·m and 0–1,400 rpm. It lists 2.0 Ah but the battery pictured is marked 3.0 Ah. The product page asks customers to confirm the actual kit; the existing AD-21V remains a separate listing.
- WALKLANDER YW-SS-WK-39: the supplied flyer lists EU 39, steel toe cap, heavy-duty leather and a rubber outsole described as oil- and slip-resistant. The page avoids claiming a safety certification.
- The INGCO image shows an example cordless drill with a 20 V marking on the pictured battery. The exact model and pack contents cannot be confirmed from that photo.
- The new cards crop out the phone and Instagram framing, the previous seller's ads and contact details, and seller stamps. Existing device pixels and manufacturer identifiers are preserved. The real OHGS badge is part of the website card.

QA

- Playback: 50 video/viewport cases; scrolling, source reuse, buffering, manual controls, replay and browser autoplay denial.
- Theme/loading: 74 page/theme cases, Kiswahili labels, loader cleanup and simulated contact form responses.
- Real Chrome playback on desktop Home, 390 px Home and 390 px Gallery; the main movie played muted, used the small source and kept only one clip loaded on initial Home view.
- Visual checks at desktop and 390 px mobile; equal card image frames, four loaded pictures, light/dark appearance and readable labels. A 320 px Kiswahili AD-18 detail page showed the correct image, browser title and WhatsApp model with no horizontal overflow.
- Catalog search for AD-18 returned only the intended product.
- Both new H.264/AAC video versions are web-ready; the autoplay/mobile file is approximately 2.7 MB, down from the 72 MB upload. Responsive photos are served locally.
